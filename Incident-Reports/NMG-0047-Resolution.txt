TICKET NUMBER: NMG-0047

AFFECTED USER:
Julissa Cooper (jcooper@NMG.com)

CORRECT JOB TITLE:
HR Payroll Specialist

CORRECT DEPARTMENT:
HR

ISSUE REPORTED:
Julissa could log in successfully but could not access HR shared resources. Her desktop policies, restrictions, and mapped drives were different from those of her HR teammates.

INVESTIGATION FINDINGS:

Julissa’s user account was incorrectly placed in the Operations OU instead of the HR OU.
Julissa was a member of the Operations-Users security group. She should have been a member of the HR-Users security group.
Under the Organization tab, Julissa’s job title was incorrectly listed as Operations Coordinator.
Her department was incorrectly listed as Operations.
Her correct job title is HR Payroll Specialist, and her correct department is HR.

ROOT CAUSE:
Julissa’s account was provisioned with incorrect organizational information. Her account was placed in the Operations OU, added to the Operations-Users security group, and given the wrong job title and department.

These errors caused Julissa to receive Operations access and policies instead of the HR access required for her role. As a result, the correct HR permissions, desktop policies, and mapped drives were not applied.

FIX APPLIED:

Moved Julissa’s account from the Operations OU to the HR OU.
Removed Julissa from the Operations-Users security group.
Added Julissa to the HR-Users security group.
Updated her job title from Operations Coordinator to HR Payroll Specialist.
Updated her department from Operations to HR.

These changes were made to ensure that Julissa’s account information and role-based access matched her HR Payroll Specialist position.

VERIFICATION:
Confirmed that Julissa’s account was located in the HR OU and that she was a member of the HR-Users security group. Verified that she was no longer a member of Operations-Users. Also confirmed that the Organization tab displayed the correct job title and department. Julissa’s Active Directory configuration now matched the established configuration for an HR employee.
